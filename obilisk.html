    • Oblilisk: Tower Of Fate

    Developed in 2026 as the capstone project for my Diploma in Screen and Media (specializing in Game Design), Oblilisk is an engaging VR dungeon crawler where you climb a tower to battle enemies. Players must utilize their magic and weapons to strategically defeat foes and conquer the final boss, offering a unique and challenging gameplay experience.

    Oblilisk is currently in development and is not yet available for public playtesting or purchase.
